# Real Life Games

Short, printable rule cheat sheets for real life games (e.g. card games). Each one prints 4-up on a single double-sided page; a 1-up version (one card per page) is also available.

## MiniBridge
MiniBridge is a simplified form of the card game *contract bridge*. It does away with bidding to focus on learning the basics.

- 4-up: [letter](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/michal-dobrogost/irl-games/main/minibridge/minibridge-rules.pdf) or [a4](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/michal-dobrogost/irl-games/main/minibridge/minibridge-rules-a4.pdf)
- 1-up: [letter](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/michal-dobrogost/irl-games/main/minibridge/minibridge-rules-1up.pdf) or [a4](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/michal-dobrogost/irl-games/main/minibridge/minibridge-rules-1up-a4.pdf)

These materials were originally based on Kitty Cooper's MiniBridge materials ([link](https://bridgeteaching.com/downloads/?category=1)) but have diverged significantly since.

## Toronto Bid Euchre
Toronto Bid Euchre is a new 4-player euchre variant targeting simple bidding and child-friendly 7 card hands. Bidding consists of a single round rewarding estimating your trick count. Within the constraints of a small hand the game was designed to reward communication between partners as you may "salt" a bid to exchange cards before the first trick. You may "pepper" a bid to play alone but the scoring was designed for this to be an occasional feature.

- 4-up: [letter](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/michal-dobrogost/irl-games/main/toronto-bid-euchre/toronto-bid-euchre-rules.pdf) or [a4](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/michal-dobrogost/irl-games/main/toronto-bid-euchre/toronto-bid-euchre-rules-a4.pdf)
- 1-up: [letter](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/michal-dobrogost/irl-games/main/toronto-bid-euchre/toronto-bid-euchre-rules-1up.pdf) or [a4](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/michal-dobrogost/irl-games/main/toronto-bid-euchre/toronto-bid-euchre-rules-1up-a4.pdf)

## Building
Each directory has its own scripts: build the PDFs with `./build.sh` and remove LaTeX intermediate files with `./clean.sh`.
