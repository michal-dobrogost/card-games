% ---------- SIDE B: SCORING ----------
\newcommand{\sideB}{%
  \raggedright
  \cardtitle{MiniBridge Scoring}

  Play continues to a predetermined goal such as 1000 points or 8 deals.\gap

  \textbf{Contracts made}\gap

  Bonuses and points are awarded to the declaring side only if they win the contract's target number of tricks, or more.\gap

  No points are won for the first 6 tricks (the book).
  Every trick won thereafter, including any beyond the contract's target, scores points according to the
  contract as follows:\gap

  \begin{tabular}{@{}l@{}l@{}}
    \makebox[0.4\linewidth][l]{No trump} & 40 points (trick 7), 30 points (tricks 8+)\\
    \makebox[0.4\linewidth][l]{Major suit (\Spade\ or \Heart)} & 30 points\\
    \makebox[0.4\linewidth][l]{Minor suit (\Diamondsuit\ or \Club)} & 20 points
  \end{tabular}\gap

  A bonus is awarded based on the level of the contract announced:\gap

  \renewcommand{\arraystretch}{1.3}
  \begin{tabular}{|l|l|l|l|l|}
    \hline
    Type & Trump suit & Level & Tricks target & Bonus \\
    \hline
    Part-score & any & 1 & 7 & 50 points \\
    \hline
    Game &
      {\renewcommand{\arraystretch}{1.0}\begin{tabular}[t]{@{}l@{}}
        No trump\\
        Major (\Spade{} or \Heart)\\
        Minor (\Diamondsuit{} or \Club)
      \end{tabular}} &
      {\renewcommand{\arraystretch}{1.0}\begin{tabular}[t]{@{}l@{}}
        3 \\
        4 \\
        5
      \end{tabular}} &
      {\renewcommand{\arraystretch}{1.0}\begin{tabular}[t]{@{}l@{}}
        9 \\
        10 \\
        11
      \end{tabular}} &
      300 points \\
    \hline
  \end{tabular}
  \gap

  \textbf{Contracts defeated}\gap

  If a contract is defeated (i.e., the target number of tricks is not reached),
  the defending side scores points instead of the declaring side. The
  defenders score for every trick short of the target:

  \gap
  \begin{tabular}{@{}l@{}l@{}}
    \makebox[0.4\linewidth][l]{Each undertrick} & 50 points\\
  \end{tabular}
  \vfill{\fontsize{5.5}{6.5}\selectfont\noindent\expandafter\url\expandafter{\footurl}}}

% ---------- SIDE A: RULES ----------
\newcommand{\sideA}{%
  \cardtitle{MiniBridge Rules}
  {\raggedright\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.5em,itemsep=0.5\baselineskip,topsep=0pt,parsep=0pt]
    \item Shuffle and deal the cards, 13 each to the 4 players.
    \item Sum and announce high-card points (HCP), clockwise, dealer starts:
      \\[0.3\baselineskip]
      \hspace*{0.4em}Ace = 4\quad King = 3\quad Queen = 2\quad Jack = 1
    \item
      Place declarer's partner's hand face up on the table as dummy.\\
      Partnership --- A team of two players sitting across from each other. \\
      Declaring side --- The partnership with the higher combined HCP. \\
      Declarer --- Partner with the higher HCP or first to announce if equal.
    \item Declarer announces the contract --- a level and choice of trump suit or no trump.
      The level is the number of tricks needed after the first 6, e.g.\ a contract of 4\Heart{} needs 10 tricks.
      Consider a trump contract if the declaring side has 8+ cards in a suit (a fit).
      The following table shows the level you may expect to make:
      \\[0.3\baselineskip]
      \begin{tabular}{|l|llll|}
        \hline
        \multirow{2}{*}{Contract suit} & \multicolumn{4}{c|}{Combined HCP} \\
        %\cline{2-5}
        & 21+ & 23+ & 25+ & 27+ \\
        \hline
        No trump                          & 1 & 2 & \textbf{3} & -- \\
        Major (\Spade{} or \Heart{})      & 2 & 3 & \textbf{4} & -- \\
        Minor (\Diamondsuit{} or \Club{}) & 2 & 3 & 4 & \textbf{5} \\
        \hline
      \end{tabular}
    \item
      The opening lead is made by the player left of declarer; others follow clockwise.
      \emph{You must follow the suit led.}
      If you can't, play a trump or any other card.
      Declarer plays from their own hand and the dummy.
    \item
      The highest trump wins the trick, otherwise the highest card of the suit led.
      Cards are ranked: A, K, Q, J, 10 ... 2.
      After each trick, the four cards are turned face down.
      The winner leads to the next trick.
    \item At the end of play, the tricks won are counted and scored.
    \item For the next hand, the dealer is the next player clockwise.
  \end{enumerate}}
}

% Dashed cut lines through the page centre; drawn on the front page only, so the
% back side prints clean. Must follow \fourup in the same paragraph.
\newcommand{\cutlines}{%
  \begin{tikzpicture}[remember picture,overlay]
    \draw[gray!60,dash pattern=on 3pt off 3pt,thin]
      (current page.north) -- (current page.south);
    \draw[gray!60,dash pattern=on 3pt off 3pt,thin]
      (current page.west) -- (current page.east);
  \end{tikzpicture}}
