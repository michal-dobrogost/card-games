# Bid Euchre
Bid Euchre is a trick-taking card game for four players in two partnerships, played with a 24-card deck. This directory contains a PDF of a Bid Euchre rules & scoring cheat sheet that prints 4-up on a single double-sided page. Download in [letter](https://github.com/michal-dobrogost/minibridge/blob/main/euchre/euchre-rules.pdf) or [a4](https://github.com/michal-dobrogost/minibridge/blob/main/euchre/euchre-rules-a4.pdf) form. A 1-up version (one card per page) is also available in [letter](https://github.com/michal-dobrogost/minibridge/blob/main/euchre/euchre-rules-1up.pdf) or [a4](https://github.com/michal-dobrogost/minibridge/blob/main/euchre/euchre-rules-1up-a4.pdf).

Bids are 1 to 6 tricks with a trump suit or no trump, bidding continues until 3 passes in a row, and the maker may call salt (a one-card swap between partners on both teams), pepper (play alone) or both. The game is played to 32 points.

Build with `./build.sh` and remove LaTeX intermediate files with `./clean.sh`.
