% ---------- SIDE A: RULES ----------
\newcommand{\sideA}{%
  \cardtitle{Thousand (1000)}
  {\raggedright
  An excellent three-player trick-taking game with changing trumps.\gap

  \begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.5em,itemsep=0.5\baselineskip,topsep=0pt,parsep=0pt]
    \item
      Deal a 24-card deck (A, K, Q, J, 10, 9 in each suit) clockwise starting left of dealer to 3 players (7 cards each) and a 3-card face down kitty.
    \item
      Starting left of dealer and going clockwise, each player passes or bids the number of points they are to make (100 minimum, 5 point increments).
      Once passed a player may not rejoin the bidding.
      Each bid must be for more points than the previous bid.
      If no one bids, the dealer must bid at least 100.
    \item
      The highest bidder takes the kitty into their hand and passes 1 card face down to every other player.
      They may revise their bid upwards.
    \item
      The highest bidder leads one card face up for the first trick; others follow clockwise.
      \emph{You must follow the suit led.} If you can't you must play a trump. If you can't play any other card.
      The highest trump wins the trick; otherwise the highest card of the suit led wins.
      The winner leads to the next trick.
    \item
      Cards are ranked and awarded points as follows (120 in full deck):\par\vspace{0.3\baselineskip}
      \hspace*{0.5em}\textbf{A}~11\qquad\textbf{10}~10\qquad\textbf{K}~4\qquad\textbf{Q}~3\qquad\textbf{J}~2\qquad\textbf{9}~0
    \item
      When you lead a Q you may announce her marriage only when you hold the same suited K in hand.
      A marriage establishes a new trump suit going forward and is awarded points as follows:\par\vspace{0.3\baselineskip}
      \hspace*{0.5em}\Heart~100\qquad\Diamondsuit~80\qquad\Club~60\qquad\Spade~40
    \item
      You score: your announced marriages and the sum points of the cards in the tricks you took.
      The highest bidder scores their bid if they made it, otherwise minus their bid.
      First to 1000 points wins.
      Scores may go below zero.
    \item For the next hand, dealer is the next player clockwise.
  \end{enumerate}}
}

% ---------- SIDE B: SCORING ----------
\newcommand{\sideB}{%
  \raggedright
  \renewcommand{\gap}{\vspace{0.5\baselineskip}}% tighter than the rules side, so it all fits
  \cardtitle{Thousand: Score}

  \textbf{Makers}\gap

  The maker and partner are the makers. The other side are the defenders.
  The contract is made if the makers win at least the number of tricks bid, otherwise it is set.
  Makers score points as follows:
  \gap

  \renewcommand{\arraystretch}{1.3}
  \begin{tabular}{|l|l|l|}
    \hline
    Contract & Made & Set (failed) \\
    \hline
    Plain & tricks won & \textminus{}tricks bid \\
    Salted & tricks won \textminus\ 1 & \textminus{}tricks bid \textminus\ 1 \\
    Peppered & tricks won + 4 & \textminus{}tricks bid \textminus\ 4 \\
    Salted \& peppered & tricks won + 2 & \textminus{}tricks bid \textminus\ 8 \\
    \hline
  \end{tabular}\gap

  \textbf{Defenders}\gap

  The defenders score 1 point for each trick they take, whether or not the contract is made.\gap

  \textbf{Examples}\gap

  \begin{tabular}{|l|l|r@{}l|l|}
    \hline
    Contract & \stackhead{Tricks won\\by makers} & \multicolumn{2}{l|}{\stackhead{Makers\\score}} & \stackhead{Defenders\\score} \\
    \hline
    Plain 6 low                & 6 & 6            &                                          & 1 \\
    Salted 5\Heart             & 6 & 5            & \ =\ \phantom{\textminus}6 \textminus\ 1 & 1 \\
    Peppered 5\Heart           & 5 & 9            & \ =\ \phantom{\textminus}5 + 4           & 2 \\
    Salted \& peppered 5\Heart & 4 & \textminus13 & \ =\ \textminus5 \textminus\ 8           & 3 \\
    \hline
  \end{tabular}\gap

  \textbf{Winning}\gap

  %The first side to reach \winscore{} points wins (about 10 hands). If both teams achieve 33 points on the same hand, the higher score count wins (if still tied then makers on last deal win). Scores can go below zero.
  First side to \winscore{} wins (about 10 hands). If both get there on the same hand, the higher score wins and makers take ties. Scores can go below zero.

  \gap
  \vfill{\fontsize{5.5}{6.5}\selectfont\noindent\expandafter\url\expandafter{\footurl}}}

      (current page.west) -- (current page.east);
  \end{tikzpicture}}
