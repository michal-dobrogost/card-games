% ---------- SIDE A: RULES ----------
\newcommand{\sideA}{%
  \cardtitle{Toronto Bid Euchre: Rules}
  {\raggedright\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.5em,itemsep=0.5\baselineskip,topsep=0pt,parsep=0pt]
    \item \textbf{Deal} 7 cards each, clockwise from dealer's left, to 4 players in 2 teams. Partners sit opposite.
      28-card deck: A, K, Q, J, 10, 9, 8.
    \item \textbf{Bid} once each, clockwise from dealer's left:
      pass, or bid tricks your side will take (1--7) plus trump, high or low
      (e.g.\ 4\Heart, 3~high, 5~low).
      Each bid must be higher.
      If all others pass, dealer must bid 4+.
      The high bidder is the maker and the high bid is the contract.
    \item
      \textbf{Calls} \emph{(beginners skip):} on a contract of 5--7 tricks, the maker may:\\
      Salt --- all players swap one card face down with their partner.\\
      Pepper --- maker plays alone (partner sits out with hand face down).\\
      Salt \& pepper --- salt first, then pepper.
    \item
      \textbf{Tricks:} maker leads one card face up; others follow clockwise.
      \emph{You must follow suit if you can}; otherwise play trump or any other card.
      Highest trump wins, else highest card of the suit led.
      The 4 cards played are turned face down and the winner leads the next trick.
    \item
      \textbf{Card ranking} depends on the contract.
      In trump, the jack of trump (right bower) is highest, the other jack of the same colour (left bower) is second highest.
      \\[0.3\baselineskip]
      {\renewcommand{\arraystretch}{1.2}%
      \begin{tabularx}{\linewidth}{|l|>{\raggedright\arraybackslash}X|}
        \hline
        Contract & Ranking, highest first \\
        \hline
        \stackhead{Trump suit (e.g.\ \Heart)} &
          J\Heart, J\Diamondsuit, A\Heart, K\Heart, Q\Heart, 10\Heart, 9\Heart, 8\Heart \\
        \hline
        High (no trump) & A, K, Q, J, 10, 9, 8 \\
        \hline
        Low (no trump) & 8, 9, 10, J, Q, K, A \\
        \hline
      \end{tabularx}}\\[0.3\baselineskip]
      \emph{The left bower (e.g. J\Diamondsuit) is a trump, not a card of its own suit.}
    \item \textbf{Tally score.} Deal passes clockwise.
  \end{enumerate}}

  \cardfooter
}

% ---------- SIDE B: SCORING ----------
\newcommand{\sideB}{%
  \raggedright
  \renewcommand{\gap}{\vspace{0.5\baselineskip}}% tighter than the rules side, so it all fits
  \cardtitle{Toronto Bid Euchre: Score}

  \textbf{Makers}\gap

  The maker and partner are the makers. The other side are the defenders.
  The contract is made if the makers win at least the number of tricks bid, otherwise it is set.
  Makers score:
  \gap

  \renewcommand{\arraystretch}{1.3}
  \begin{tabular}{|l|l|l|}
    \hline
    Contract & Made & Set (failed) \\
    \hline
    Plain & tricks won & \textminus{}tricks bid \\
    Salted & tricks won \textminus\ 1 & \textminus{}tricks bid \textminus\ 1 \\
    Peppered & tricks won + 4 & \textminus{}tricks bid \textminus\ 4 \\
    Salted \& peppered & tricks won + 2 & \textminus{}tricks bid \textminus\ 8 \\
    \hline
  \end{tabular}\gap

  \textbf{Defenders}\gap

  Defenders score 1 per trick taken, contract made or set.\gap

  \textbf{Examples}\gap

  \begin{tabular}{|l|r@{}l|l|}
    \hline
    Contract & \multicolumn{2}{l|}{\stackhead{Makers\\score}} & \stackhead{Defenders\\score} \\
    \hline
    Plain 6 low                & 6            &                                          & 1 \\
    Salted 5\Heart             & 5            & \ =\ \phantom{\textminus}6 \textminus\ 1 & 1 \\
    Peppered 5\Heart           & 9            & \ =\ \phantom{\textminus}5 + 4           & 2 \\
    Salted \& peppered 5\Heart & \textminus13 & \ =\ \textminus5 \textminus\ 8           & 3 \\
    \hline
  \end{tabular}\gap

  \textbf{Winning}\gap

  %The first side to reach \winscore{} points wins (about 10 hands). If both teams achieve 33 points on the same hand, the higher score count wins (if still tied then makers on last deal win). Scores can go below zero.
  First side to \winscore{} wins ($\approx$10 hands). If both reach \winscore{} on the same hand, the higher score wins; ties go to the makers. Scores can go negative.

  \cardfooter
}
